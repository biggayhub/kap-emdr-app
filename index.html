Session guide

Your last session is saved

A spoken companion for an at-home ketamine-assisted psychotherapy session, lying in bed. After one tap to start and one tap once you’ve taken your medicine, it runs on its own.

This guide doesn't replace your clinician. Take your medicine exactly as they prescribed, and follow their instructions about food, other medications and having someone with you.
