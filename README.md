# KAP & EMDR Session Guide

A personal hobby project: a spoken, gently paced companion for an at-home ketamine-assisted psychotherapy (KAP) session, plus a self-guided EMDR-style exercise.

> **This is not medical advice, therapy, or treatment, and it is not a medical device.** It was made for personal use and shared with friends for feedback. It has not been reviewed by any clinician and is not affiliated with any clinic or provider. It gives no dosing or medication guidance. Use entirely at your own risk; it is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. Adults 18+ only. See [disclaimer.html](disclaimer.html) for the full disclaimer.
>
> **In crisis?** Call or text **988** (US Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). In an emergency, call **911**.

Open the GitHub Pages site on your phone and add it to your home screen. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; it is stored only in your own browser. Voices are AI-generated.
